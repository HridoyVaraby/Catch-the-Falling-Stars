How to Play

Basic Controls

  • Use Left and Right Arrow Keys to move the basket
  • Alternatively, use your Mouse to control the basket movement

Game Elements

  • 🌟 Gold Stars - Basic points (1 point)
  • 🌟 Blue Stars - Double points (2 points)
  • 🌟 Red Stars - Triple points (3 points)
  • 🌟 Purple Stars - Double points (2 points)
  • 💖 Hearts - Extra life
  • ⚠️ Space Debris - Reduces points (-2 points)
  • ☠️ Dangerous Debris - Heavy point loss (-4 points)

Tips & Strategies

  • Focus on catching valuable stars while avoiding debris
  • Keep an eye on your lives - catch hearts when your lives are low
  • The game speed increases as you score more points
  • Try to maintain a steady position and make calculated movements
